A different question

Creativity is not one ability.

Many people conclude that they are not creative because their strengths do not resemble a narrow stereotype of the artist, inventor, or entrepreneur.

The InnerCube Framework recognizes that people contribute to creativity in different but complementary ways. It does not ask how creative you are. It helps you explore the pattern through which your creativity naturally begins.

The question is not whether you are creative.

The question is how.
