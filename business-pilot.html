THE LEARNING CYCLE

From uncertainty to something your team can learn from.

Creative Intelligence is designed to work around the business you're already running—not take your team away from it.

01 — EXPLORE

Find what is worth learning from.

We begin with a 30–60 minute remote session. Together we clarify the challenge, decision, goal or opportunity that matters most.

02 — PERSPECTIVES

Let every person see the challenge from where they stand.

Team members independently share what they see happening, what might be causing it, what they would try, what they expect, and what the team may be assuming.

03 — TEAM INTELLIGENCE

See how your organization is thinking.

Creative Intelligence brings the perspectives together, revealing agreements, differences, overlooked observations, assumptions and questions worth testing. It also identifies the Creative Intelligence modes shaping the group's approach—and perspectives that may be underused.

04 — EXPERIMENT

Choose something achievable to test.

Your team chooses one decision or experiment, records what it expects to happen and identifies what evidence would indicate whether the idea is working.

05 — REALITY

Return to work.

The experiment happens naturally inside the business. It might take days, weeks or longer. Your team continues doing its normal work while reality begins providing evidence.

06 — LEARN

Turn what happened into what comes next.

At the review point, the outcome is recorded. Creative Intelligence compares expectations with reality, identifies what the organization learned, and reveals the question worth exploring next.

PERSPECTIVE → DECISION → ACTION → OUTCOME → LEARNING → NEXT DECISION
