The four foundations

Four perspectives for coaching yourself.

These are capacities to practice, not fixed personality types. Each offers a different question when you feel uncertain, stuck, or ready for something new.

01

Dreamer

What else could be possible? Give imagination room before narrowing your choices.

02

Observer

What is happening? Notice the situation, your assumptions, and what you may be missing.

03

Alchemist

What can change through practice? Turn experience into a small experiment and learn from it.

04

Visionary

What matters enough to move toward? Connect insight with a direction you choose.
