Participant learning journey Fictional example

Richard · Goal: develop greater confidence in making important decisions.

Perspective: Perhaps good decisions do not require certainty. Small independent decisions could become opportunities to learn.
Experiment: Make one low-risk decision independently and observe uncertainty, reassurance-seeking, and the outcome.
Reflection: “I made a small decision independently without asking anyone for reassurance. Although I initially felt uncertain, I discovered that I could move forward without being completely sure.”
Learning: “Confidence does not require certainty. It can develop through making decisions, observing results, and learning from experience.”
Next move: Make three independent decisions over the next week, record reasoning, and reflect on outcomes.

This example illustrates self-reported learning, not independently verified behavior change or clinical outcomes.
