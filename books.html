Books

Books Exploring Creativity, Transformation, and Human Development

A collection of works exploring imagination, meaning, creativity, second-order change, and the future of humanity in an age of artificial intelligence.

Books are conversations across time.

Each explores a different dimension of Creative Intelligence.

The Alchemy of Change

An exploration of transformation through the lens of systems thinking, awareness, and second-order change.

The book examines why lasting change rarely occurs through force alone and how deeper transformation emerges when individuals and groups become aware of the assumptions shaping their experience.

Painting to Poetry

A reflection on creativity, meaning, and the relationship between human expression and artificial intelligence.

Using artworks and poetry, the book explores the uniquely human dimensions of perception, interpretation, and creative expression.

The Last Human Creator

A speculative novel exploring creativity, consciousness, and humanity’s relationship with intelligent machines.

Set across multiple lifetimes and futures, the story asks what remains uniquely human when machines become capable of replicating nearly everything else.

Works in Progress

Several additional works continue to explore themes related to Creative Intelligence and human development.

The Intuition Barrier

A practical and philosophical exploration of overthinking, intuition, perception, and the stories we create about ourselves and the world.

Creative Intelligence

A developing framework for understanding imagination, contemplation, transformation, purpose, and collective awareness in the age of AI.

Future Projects

Additional works exploring education, creativity, language learning, and the future of human development.

Technology may change how we work. Stories help shape who we become.

A Shared Theme

Although these books explore different subjects, they share a common concern:

How do human beings remain creative, meaningful, and fully alive in a rapidly changing world?

That question lies at the heart of Creative Intelligence World.
