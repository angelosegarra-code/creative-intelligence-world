Professional Profile

Angelo Segarra

Angelo Segarra is an author, educator, facilitator, artist, and whole systems designer exploring how artificial intelligence can support creativity, reflection, learning, and human flourishing without replacing human judgment.

His work integrates systems thinking, reflective practice, storytelling, philosophy, the creative arts, participatory learning, and AI-assisted development to help individuals and organizations navigate complexity with greater imagination, awareness, and purpose.

Human-Centered AI

“AI should cultivate deeper questions, not merely provide faster answers.”

My work explores how AI can expand human creativity rather than replace it. I am particularly interested in designing systems that preserve curiosity, reflection, imagination, and human judgment.

Through books, educational experiences, and digital projects, I investigate how people and intelligent systems can participate together in solving meaningful problems while strengthening, rather than diminishing, human agency.
