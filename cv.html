Professional Profile

Whole Systems Designer and learning experience designer exploring how artificial intelligence can expand human creativity, reflective practice, and collective intelligence. With more than fourteen years of international teaching experience and a Master’s degree in Whole Systems Design, my work investigates how people learn, create, and flourish alongside intelligent technologies.

I design educational experiences that combine systems thinking, creativity, narrative, reflection, and emerging AI tools. My work spans curriculum design, immersive learning environments, public educational platforms, books, and cinematic storytelling.
