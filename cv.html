Professional Profile

Angelo Segarra

Angelo Segarra is an author, educator, artist, facilitator, and whole systems designer exploring the intersection of creativity, consciousness, transformation, and human-centered artificial intelligence.

His work brings together systems thinking, reflective practice, storytelling, philosophy, the creative arts, and participatory learning to help individuals and groups navigate change with greater imagination, awareness, and purpose.

● Approach

“AI should cultivate deeper questions, not merely provide faster answers.”

Angelo’s approach is participatory, experiential, and systems-oriented. He does not begin by imposing solutions. He helps individuals and groups notice the assumptions, relationships, and recurring patterns shaping their experience.

When those patterns become visible, creativity and collective intelligence can emerge in new ways.
