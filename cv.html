Professional Profile

I am an author, educator, artist, facilitator, and whole systems designer with more than fourteen years of international teaching experience. My work explores how creativity, reflection, systems thinking, and human-centered artificial intelligence can help people and organizations respond to complexity with greater imagination, awareness, and purpose.

I design and facilitate participatory learning experiences that bring people into meaningful dialogue with one another. These experiences combine reflective inquiry, creative practice, storytelling, group conversation, systems awareness, and emerging AI tools.

My work spans books, retreats, university education, workshops, public essays, creative frameworks, curriculum design, and cinematic storytelling.

Facilitation & Collaboration

I am available to facilitate groups, workshops, retreats, learning programs, and conversations for universities, schools, organizations, creative teams, and communities.

My facilitation focuses on creating environments where participants can slow down, examine the patterns shaping their decisions, engage uncertainty constructively, and discover new possibilities together.

Learning & Facilitation Philosophy

“AI should cultivate deeper questions, not merely provide faster answers.”

Effective learning environments do more than transfer information. They create the conditions for curiosity, participation, reflection, experimentation, and dialogue.

My role as a facilitator is not to impose conclusions, but to help a group notice the assumptions, relationships, and patterns shaping its experience. When these structures become visible, new forms of intelligence and collaboration can emerge.
