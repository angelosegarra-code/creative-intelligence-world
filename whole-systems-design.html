The approach

Change begins with understanding the system.

Leadership, communication, technology, culture, and strategy influence one another. Addressing one part without understanding its relationships to the others can create unexpected consequences.

Whole Systems Design begins by observing those relationships, listening to different perspectives, questioning assumptions, and identifying manageable experiments. The aim is not to impose a predetermined answer, but to help an organization learn its way forward.

A focused engagement

The Whole Systems Discovery Project

A three-week, remotely delivered assessment of one meaningful organizational challenge.

Week 01

Discovery & Observation

Leadership conversation, review of selected documents, up to three stakeholder interviews, and initial mapping of relationships and assumptions.

Week 02

Perspectives & Possibilities

A facilitated 90-minute stakeholder session to explore differing perspectives, recurring patterns, and overlooked possibilities.

Week 03

Direction & Learning

Identify two or three practical experiments, observable signals, and a 30–60-day learning roadmap. Conclude with a leadership review.

About the consultant

Angelo Segarra, MA

His interdisciplinary background combines systems thinking, creative practice, and more than a decade of international education experience.
