What Is Creative Intelligence?

Creative Intelligence is the capacity to imagine possibilities, recognize patterns, create meaning, adapt to change, and intentionally shape the future.

In an AI-powered world, these capacities become more important, not less. Creative Intelligence integrates imagination and insight, intuition and analysis, meaning and action.
