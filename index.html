The Opportunity

Every learner thinks differently.

Yet education has traditionally been designed for the average learner. Artificial Intelligence now makes it possible to create learning that adapts to each individual instead of expecting every individual to adapt to the system.

01

One Curriculum

Different learners are expected to follow the same educational path regardless of how they naturally think.

02

One Pace

Some learners become bored. Others become discouraged. Many assume they simply are not intelligent.

03

One Definition

When education measures only one kind of intelligence, many forms of creativity remain invisible.

What if education adapted to the learner... instead of asking the learner to adapt to education?
