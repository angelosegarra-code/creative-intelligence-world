The InnerCube Framework

Four complementary ways of participating in creativity. Every learner possesses each of these capacities, but develops them in unique ways.

Dreamer

Imagination. Possibility. Curiosity. The capacity to generate ideas that do not yet exist.

Observer

Attention. Reflection. Awareness. Learning begins by noticing what others overlook.

Alchemist

Transformation. Experimentation. Growth. Ideas become meaningful through experience.

Visionary

Purpose. Direction. Future thinking. Creativity becomes contribution.

Every learning journey begins with reflection. From there, AI helps identify patterns, strengths, opportunities, and personalized next steps.

1. Reflection

Learners respond to thoughtful questions about how they approach learning, creativity, and problem solving.

2. AI Analysis

The platform analyzes relationships across responses rather than simply assigning a score.

3. Personalized Journey

Instead of a label, learners receive a customized learning pathway designed specifically for them.
