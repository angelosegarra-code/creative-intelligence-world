A framework for exploration

Meet the InnerCube.

The InnerCube brings four creative perspectives into conversation. Each offers a different way to encounter a question, a challenge, or a possibility.

These are fluid capacities, rather than fixed personality types. You may begin with a familiar perspective and discover something new by looking through another.

Sometimes growth begins when the way we see the question changes.

The Creative Intelligence Coach supports this process with questions and reflection. Your experience gives it meaning; your choices shape what happens.

01 / QUESTION

Bring what matters.

A creative project, a personal change, or a shared team challenge.

02 / EXPLORE

Shift perspectives.

Look through the Dreamer, Observer, Alchemist, and Visionary.

03 / EXPERIMENT

Try a possibility.

Choose a small action that helps you explore a new direction.

04 / REFLECT

Learn and return.

Notice what changes. Revisit the question and continue exploring.
