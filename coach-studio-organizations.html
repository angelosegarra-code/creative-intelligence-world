The challenge

What happens between meetings?

A student leaves an advising appointment with a goal. A veteran explores a career transition. An employee agrees to try a new approach. The next meeting may be weeks away. Coach Studio is being developed to help connect those conversations with participant-led action, reflection, and learning.

One learning system. Three distinct roles.

Participants

Choose goals, explore the four Creative Intelligence perspectives, try small actions, and decide what to share.

Professionals

Review authorized learning activity, suggest challenges, and support continued development between appointments.

Organizations

Proposed program administration and privacy-conscious aggregate reporting, without automatic access to private client reflections.

Explore a proposed founding institutional pilot

A pilot could begin with a small group—for example, five professionals and up to 25 participants over 60–90 days. Scope, pricing, safeguards, training, and evaluation criteria would be agreed before any live deployment. This is an illustrative pilot design, not a currently bookable package.

1. Discover

Map the program's existing advising or coaching workflow and define an appropriate nonclinical use case.

2. Prepare

Confirm permissions, consent, security, support, and the functional capabilities required before participant onboarding.

3. Learn

Evaluate usability, activity completion, professional feedback, and participant-reported learning without overstating outcomes.

Responsible scope: The current Coach Studio is a private technical pilot. It is not approved for clinical therapy, medical records, protected health information, or sensitive student records. Institutional use requires additional engineering, security, accessibility, and applicable legal review.
