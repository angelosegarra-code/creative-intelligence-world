Speaking & Workshops

Creative Intelligence for the Age of AI

Keynotes, workshops, and facilitated conversations exploring creativity, human development, systems change, and the future of human potential.

Artificial Intelligence is transforming what machines can do.

Creative Intelligence explores what humans can become.

Why These Conversations Matter

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping education, work, leadership, and creativity at an unprecedented pace. While technology continues to expand what machines can accomplish, the deeper challenge facing individuals and organizations is learning how to cultivate the human capacities that technology cannot replace.

Creativity, imagination, reflection, meaning-making, and the ability to navigate uncertainty are becoming increasingly important in a world defined by rapid change.

My work explores these capacities through the lens of Whole Systems Design, creativity, personal transformation, leadership development, and emerging technologies. The goal is not simply to understand change, but to participate in it more consciously and creatively.

Whether speaking to university students, educators, leadership teams, innovation groups, or community organizations, I help audiences explore practical ways to remain adaptive, purposeful, and deeply human in an age of increasing complexity.

Core Topics

Speaking Themes

Creativity in the Age of AI

Human Potential

As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly capable, what remains uniquely human? This presentation explores creativity as a participatory process and examines how human beings can work alongside intelligent technologies while preserving meaning, imagination, and purpose.

The Four Foundations of Creative Intelligence

Development Framework

A practical framework based on four developmental capacities: Dreamer, Observer, Alchemist, and Visionary. Participants explore how imagination, reflection, transformation, and vision contribute to personal and organizational growth.

The Alchemy of Change

Systems Awareness

Why do people and organizations struggle with change? Drawing on systems thinking and Whole Systems Design, this presentation explores the difference between first-order and second-order change and how genuine transformation becomes possible.

Human Creativity and the Future

Future Possibilities

A hopeful exploration of humanity's evolving relationship with technology, creativity, and collective intelligence. What capacities will help individuals and communities flourish in the decades ahead?

About Angelo Segarra

Angelo Segarra is an author, educator, facilitator, artist, and Whole Systems Design practitioner whose work explores creativity, transformation, human development, and the evolving relationship between human intelligence and artificial intelligence.

He holds a Master's Degree in Whole Systems Design from Antioch University and has spent more than thirteen years teaching and facilitating learning experiences throughout Asia.

His work bridges creativity, systems thinking, personal development, education, and emerging technologies, helping individuals and organizations navigate complexity while remaining connected to meaning, imagination, and purpose.

Angelo is the author of The Alchemy of Change, Painting to Poetry, The Creative Path, and the forthcoming novel The Last Human Creator.

He has served as an Honorary Researcher in Sculpture and has exhibited work in museums in China. Through Creative Intelligence World and Archetype Retreats Bali, he continues to explore creativity as a pathway for personal and collective transformation.
