Creative Intelligence for a Changing World

Keynotes, workshops, and conversations on creativity, transformation, leadership, second-order change, and human development in the age of AI.

Artificial Intelligence is transforming what machines can do.

Creative Intelligence explores what humans can become.

Why These Conversations Matter

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping work, education, creativity, and organizational life. As machines become more capable, the deeper question is not only how we use new tools, but how we develop the human capacities needed to thrive alongside them.

My work explores the capacities that remain uniquely human: imagination, contemplation, transformation, purpose, and collective awareness.

Drawing on decades of experience in facilitation, Whole Systems Design, creativity, education, and the arts, I help audiences think more deeply about change while discovering practical ways to remain creative, adaptive, and meaningfully human.

About Angelo Segarra

Angelo Segarra is an artist, author, educator, facilitator, and Whole Systems Design practitioner whose work explores creativity, transformation, and human development.

His background includes decades of work in education, creativity, facilitation, cross-cultural learning, and the arts. His books include The Alchemy of Change, Painting to Poetry, and The Last Human Creator.
